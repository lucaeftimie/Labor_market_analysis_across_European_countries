workforce development ~ economic development

economic development = process by which the economic well-being and quality of life of a nation region local community or an individaul are improved according to targeted goals and objectives.

workforce development = comprehensive approach to preparing individuals for sucess in the labor market while ensuring that businesses have acecess to the talent they need to grow and thrive.
	-- connects people to opportunity
	-- focus: upskilling workers, closing equity gaps, responding to employer needs, adapting to the evolving demands of the labor market
